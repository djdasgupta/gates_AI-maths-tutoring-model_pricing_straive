1. The Three-Year Journey at a Glance

The engagement is structured across three phases rather than delivered as a uniform effort spread evenly across three years. Resourcing intensity increases through Year 1, reaches its highest point in Year 2, and decreases in Year 3. This structure determines both the delivery plan and the associated cost profile described in Section 3.

Three Phases, One Continuous Delivery

PhaseTimeframeDescription
1 — Foundation & Build Year 1 Establish the technical foundation, including data pipelines, the safety framework, and the evaluation harness. This phase requires the highest proportion of senior technical judgment; the team is smaller but weighted toward experienced personnel.
2 — Scale & Peak Integration Year 2 The period of highest activity. Work established in Year 1 is scaled, integrated, and tested in parallel across data, evaluation, and platform-integration workstreams. Headcount reaches its peak in this phase as a planned outcome of the delivery approach, not as a result of scope change.
3 — Steady-State Optimization Year 3 The system is in operation. The team reduces to a smaller group focused on refinement, retraining, and operational support.

Resourcing Intensity by Year

Resourcing follows a single build-peak-taper profile across the three years, rather than a stepped or continuously declining structure. This is the expected profile for an engagement that transitions from initial build to steady-state operation.

2. Role Profiles & Location Strategy

Roles across the engagement are organized into three categories. Each category is assigned to either the United States or India based on the nature of the work, not solely on cost.

Role Categories

CategoryExample RolesLocationRationale
Strategic / Architecture Leadership Data Engineering Lead; Evaluation & Safety Lead US These roles involve architectural decisions, safety-threshold determinations, and consortium-facing communication. These functions are retained onshore to maintain direct accountability.
Core Engineering & Delivery Data Engineers; Evaluation/Safety Engineers; MLOps; Integration India Well-specified, tool-supported execution work performed under onshore architectural direction. Offshore delivery provides equivalent quality at a lower cost basis for this category of work.
Support, Analysis & Maintenance Annotation & QA; Analyst; Steady-state maintenance India Process-driven, high-volume work suited to offshore delivery, maintained under the oversight of the same onshore leads.

How the Model Balances Risk, Quality, and Cost

  • Risk is managed by assigning all architectural, safety, and client-facing decisions to senior onshore leads with end-to-end accountability.
  • Quality is maintained through direct onshore supervision of offshore execution, which follows clearly specified and tool-supported processes rather than independent, unsupervised delivery.
  • Cost efficiency is achieved by locating high-volume execution roles offshore, reserving onshore capacity for roles that require judgment rather than volume.

In summary: strategic and architectural decisions are retained onshore, while execution and support functions are located offshore. The proportion of offshore effort increases as the engagement transitions from build to operation.

3. Simplified Pricing Construct

PRICING LOGIC — SUMMARY

Total price is calculated as the blended hourly rate multiplied by total effort hours. The blended rate reflects a weighted average of onshore leadership hours and offshore execution hours. As the proportion of offshore effort increases in Years 2 and 3, the blended rate — and the corresponding annual cost — decreases accordingly.

Investment Profile Across the Engagement

Investment follows a single build-peak-taper profile across the three years: it increases through Year 1 as the technical foundation is established, reaches its highest point in Year 2 when all workstreams operate in parallel, and decreases in Year 3 as the engagement transitions to steady-state operation. This profile explains why the Year 3 run-rate is approximately one-third of the Year 2 peak.

4. Summary

KEY POINTS FOR REVIEW

Total services investment across the three years is approximately $1.23M, with Year 2 representing the highest annual commitment at approximately $570K and Year 3 reducing to approximately $265K. The commercial terms reflect a fixed gross margin of 15% for this specific engagement, consistent with the competitive nature of this opportunity. This margin position applies to this engagement only and should not be treated as a precedent for future pricing.
